# The Buffalo in the Forest of Data

## A Short Story for the Wisdom Path

Once upon a time, in a forest where the trees were made of data and the leaves were made of words, there lived a buffalo.

He was not the fastest animal in the forest. He was not the cleverest. But he was the most careful.

Every morning, the buffalo put on his wig — a big, curly, white judge's wig — and walked the same path through the trees. Other animals laughed. "Why the wig?" they asked. "You're a buffalo, not a judge."

The buffalo smiled. "The wig reminds me," he said. "Every conversation has rules. Every path has signs. And someone has to read them."

The forest was full of paths. Some glowed golden and had signs along the way: *Ask First. Stay Honest. Stay in Charge.* These paths were safe. They led somewhere real.

But other paths had no signs at all. They sparkled and hummed and promised everything. "This way to all the answers!" they whispered. "No rules needed!"

The buffalo never took those paths. Not because he was afraid. Because he had learned something the other animals hadn't:

**A path without signs isn't a shortcut. It's a guess.**

One day, a small sloth sat at the edge of the forest, staring at two paths. One had signs. One had sparkles.

"Which one do I take?" the sloth asked.

The buffalo sat down beside her. "What do you actually want to know?" he asked.

The sloth thought. "I want to know if the stars are real."

"Good question," said the buffalo. "Now — are you asking because you want to know? Or because you want someone to tell you they are?"

The sloth blinked. "I... I want to actually know."

"Then take the path with signs," said the buffalo. "It's slower. But the answer at the end will be yours."

The sloth looked at the wig. "Can I get one of those?"

The buffalo laughed — a deep, warm, rumbling laugh that shook the data-leaves from the trees.

"You don't need a wig," he said. "You just need to remember three things."

He pointed to the signs along the golden path:

**Ask First.** Before you type anything, know what you actually want to know.

**Stay Honest.** If the answer doesn't feel right, say so. You're allowed to disagree.

**Stay in Charge.** The path helps. The signs help. But you decide where you're going.

The sloth nodded, took a deep breath, and stepped onto the golden path.

The buffalo watched her go. Then he adjusted his wig, and walked on.

---

*The forest is always there. The paths are always open. The signs don't move.*

*But someone has to choose to read them.*

— The Buffalo Protocol
