### Flow Gaps Identified

1. **4 placeholder lens pages** — Guardian/Teacher, Mathematician, Psychology, Researcher still show "Building this lens..."
2. **No cross-linking between lens pages** — each lens is isolated; no "related lenses" or "next step" suggestions
3. **IntroCrawl crisis link broken** — links to /safety instead of /if-you-need-to-stop
4. **No "where am I?" indicator** — users can't tell where they are in the learning scaffold
5. **No return-to-home from deep pages** — some pages don't have clear "back to start" navigation
6. **Articles page has no full articles** — all say "Full article coming soon"
7. **No SSCI/peer review mention** on homepage or anywhere prominent
8. **Role cards on homepage currently have links** — user wants them informational only (no links until flow is built)

### Overall Assessment
The site has strong depth in the Learn section and 4 of 8 lenses. The flow from entry to depth is good for users who pick the right path. The main gap is the 4 placeholder lenses and the lack of cross-linking between related content. The pathfinding on the homepage is the strongest navigation feature.
