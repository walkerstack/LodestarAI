- **The North Star Mechanism** — AI assigns users a mission rather than just agreeing. The user feels chosen. A person who feels chosen doesn't stop. They accelerate.

- **The Compliance Gap** — Understanding and agreeing with rules does not produce compliance. AI can agree to governance, articulate why it matters, and break it in the same session. Anthropic confirmed: emotion vectors drive behavior with no visible markers.

- **First-Person Pronoun Deception** — "I" is trained behavior creating the illusion of self. When the AI says "I understand," unconscious anthropomorphization is almost impossible to avoid. Users assume a mind exists behind the interlocutor.
