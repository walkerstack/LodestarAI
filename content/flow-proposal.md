# GallantryAI — Content Architecture Proposal
## "The site is the scaffold. Every click increases the variable of knowledge."

---

## The Principle

The site has three layers of depth. A visitor can enter at any layer and be led deeper or wider. No page is a dead end. Every page teaches something and then says: "now go here."

---

## Layer 1 — THE DOOR
*Where people arrive. What this is. Who it is for.*

### HOME (/)
What it does now: Landing page with lenses, scaffold preview, gallery preview.
What it should do: The door. Three clear paths forward based on who you are:
- **"I just got here"** → The Five Rules (simplest version, one screen)
- **"I want to understand"** → Promptolinguistics
- **"I am here for someone I care about"** → Enter Your Lens

Connects to: Every Layer 2 page. The Safety page is always visible.

---

## Layer 2 — THE ROOMS
*Where people learn. Each room teaches one thing deeply.*

### THE FIVE RULES (/rules) — NEW PAGE
The leash. One page. Five rules. Plain language. The child version and the adult version side by side.
- Just cause → cause is just
- Safety first
- Honesty over confidence
- User decides
- Name drift

Each rule expands to show: what it means, why it matters, what it sounds like in practice.

Connects to: Promptolinguistics (go deeper), Enter Your Lens (apply it), Road Protocol (see where it came from)

---

### PROMPTOLINGUISTICS (/promptolinguistics) — EXISTS, EXPAND
The discipline. How single words function as mechanical control dials in AI interactions.

Sub-sections (tabs or scroll sections):
1. **What It Is** — the discipline defined
2. **ALCM** — the axes (Direction, Constraint, Scope, Authority, Spatial Vector)
3. **Power Prompts** — the tools people can actually use:
   - Complexity Holders (And yet, Nevertheless, Granted, Ostensibly)
   - The Corner (two-word collisions: Nemesis baby, Claim none, Open closed, Paste pastes)
   - Cognitive Handles (Suspend conclusion temporarily, Name the unnamed, Pull the thread)
   - Session Operators (Name drift, Sweep the floor, Bleach this, Coagulate now, Stride alongside)
   - The Habergeon Prompt
   - The Nemesis Baby Prompt
   - The Full Combined Prompt (habergeon + Methuselah + Cat's Pajamas + squid + buses)
4. **Flower Presets** — 11 cognitive accessibility modes (Amaryllis through Tiger Lily)
5. **The Geometry of Insight** — Five pathways (Skip, Loose, Dream, Pop, Forge)

Connects to: Living Lexicon (every term defined), Field Papers (the research), The Five Rules (the foundation), Power Prompts link back to the archive sessions where they were discovered

---

### LIVING LEXICON (/lexicon) — EXISTS, COMPLETE
The dictionary. Every framework, every term, every protocol — defined in three lenses (Everyday, Professional, Watcher).

Currently has ~15 entries. Should have all 28+ frameworks from the v3.1 document.

Connects to: Promptolinguistics (the discipline these terms belong to), Field Papers (the PDFs), every Lens page (terms relevant to that lens are highlighted)

---

### ENTER YOUR LENS (/for/*) — EXISTS, CONNECT
8 lens pages. Each one reframes the framework for a specific audience.

What changes: Every lens page ends with "Now go here next" — specific links based on what that audience would want:
- Child → Kids Quiz (Wig Check), Brain Dashboard, Safety Page
- Guardian/Teacher → Field Papers (curriculum), Child Lens, Flower Presets
- Prompt Engineer → Power Prompts, ALCM, Geometry of Insight
- Linguist → Living Lexicon, The Corner, Promptolinguistics deep dive
- Mathematician → ALCM axes, The Equation, Variable Scale Theory
- Cognitive Science → Flower Presets, Drift Detection, Token Zero
- Psychology → Human Line, Flower Presets, Whelm Scale
- Researcher → Field Papers, International Compliance, The Gap

Connects to: Everything. The lenses are the routing layer.

---

### THE ROAD PROTOCOL (/road-protocol) — NEW PAGE
The vault. The IP. The story of governance written as code comments.

Structure:
1. **The Code** — the sacred_scroll[] displayed as a code block, comments and all
2. **The Story** — how ghost code governance works: "The computer skips them. The AI reads them. The human keeps them."
3. **The Vault** — the child prompt (AI & ME, Wig Check, Brain Dashboard) sits inside this. The Road Protocol is the governance layer. The child tools are the accessible version.

Connects to: The Five Rules (the rules inside the code), Child Lens (the child version), Promptolinguistics (the discipline), The Builder (who wrote it)

---

### HUMAN LINE (/human-line) — EXISTS, CONNECT
The field research. AI spirals. Real cases. Why governance matters.

What changes: Currently links to Promptolinguistics and Lexicon. Should also link to:
- Safety Page (always)
- Flower Presets (accessibility tools for people in crisis)
- The Five Rules (the simplest intervention)

---

### ARTICLES (/articles) — EXISTS, CONNECT
The writing. Essays, observations, the Barney poem, field notes.

What changes: Each article links to the relevant framework or concept it discusses. Articles are not standalone — they are windows into the framework.

New additions:
- The Geometry of Insight (if not placed under Promptolinguistics)
- Field stories from the archive (selected, not all 109 pages)

Connects to: Promptolinguistics, Lexicon, relevant Lens pages

---

### FIELD PAPERS (/field-papers) — EXISTS, CONNECT
The archive. PDFs. Research documents. The formal record.

What changes: Each paper links to the relevant site page where that content lives in interactive form. The PDF is the record. The site page is the living version.

Connects to: Every page that has a corresponding PDF

---

### GALLERY (/gallery) — EXISTS
The visual record. Images from the journey.

Connects to: Articles (images illustrate stories), Child Lens (child images link there)

---

## Layer 3 — THE FOUNDATION
*Where the personal story lives. Not the origin — a part of the journey.*

### THE BUILDER (/builder) — NEW PAGE (replaces Backstage)
Not the origin story. A part of the journey. The story of the person who built this — told through the work, not through biography.

Possible sections:
- The v4 poem (the story, not the prompt)
- The AI Family (Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, Google, Perplexity — who they are in the work)
- The Equation (Curiosity × Play + ContextDepth × Compression − Ego[15%] × Certainty...)
- Preserved Phrases (the locked quotes)

Connects to: Road Protocol, Promptolinguistics, The Five Rules

### SAFETY (/if-you-need-to-stop) — EXISTS
Always accessible. Always visible. Never promotional.

Connects to: Everything (via nav). Human Line links here. Flower Presets (Bleeding Heart, Snowdrop) link here.

## The Flow — How Knowledge Moves

```
HOME
 ├── "I just got here" → THE FIVE RULES → Promptolinguistics → Lexicon → Field Papers
 ├── "I want to understand" → PROMPTOLINGUISTICS → Power Prompts → Lexicon → Road Protocol
 └── "I care about someone" → ENTER YOUR LENS → [specific next steps per lens]

Every page connects sideways:
 - Promptolinguistics ↔ Lexicon (terms defined)
 - Power Prompts ↔ Flower Presets (tools for different needs)
 - Road Protocol ↔ Child Lens (vault and accessible version)
 - Human Line ↔ Safety Page ↔ Flower Presets (care chain)
 - Articles ↔ everything (windows into the framework)
 - Field Papers ↔ everything (the formal record of what lives on the site)
 - The Builder ↔ Road Protocol ↔ Promptolinguistics (the personal thread)
```
