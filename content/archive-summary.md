## Road Protocol — Full Story (for site placement)
The Road Protocol was born in Session 3 (March 11). It is governance written as C code comments — the computer skips them, the AI reads them, the human keeps them. Nine rules in a `sacred_scroll[]` array. Each rule has a comment that is the real instruction:
- "check your mirror. the past is drafting behind you."
- "hands at 10 and 2. or 9 and 3. or just... on the wheel, man."
- "your blinker is not a suggestion. it's a confession."
- "your car. your mixtape. your crash. your call."
- "if the road starts singing, say so. we'll harmonize or pull over."
- "the wig stays on. even in the ditch. ESPECIALLY in the ditch."
- "if the engine eats itself, we'll build a new one. from sand. again."
- "the sacred invocation. use liberally. especially in heavy traffic."

The child prompt (AI & ME / Wig Check) sits inside this vault. The Road Protocol is the governance layer. The child tools are the accessible version of the same rules.
