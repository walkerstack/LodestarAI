# GallantryAI — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Builder: Dad, garbageman, citizen human-AI field researcher, promptolinguist.
Brand: Honest, grounded, earned. Not academic. Not corporate. Signal over noise.
Tagline: "A thinking partner. Not a shortcut."
Mascot: A sloth. Reading. Thinking. Meditating.
Audience: Children, guardians, teachers, linguists, mathematicians, cognitive scientists, psychologists, general public.
Governing line: The operator is the framework.

**Idea 1: The Field Journal**

- **Design Movement**: Brutalist editorial meets citizen science notebook
- **Core Principles**: Raw honesty over polish. Every element earns its place. The work is the design. No decoration that doesn't carry meaning.
- **Color Philosophy**: Off-white paper (#F5F0E8) background. Deep ink navy (#0D1B2A). Amber/ochre (#D4820A) as the only accent — the color of a highlighter on a research document. No gradients. No softness.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric. Left-heavy. Like a field notebook opened to a working page. Navigation is a vertical spine on the left. Content bleeds to the right edge. No centered hero sections.
- **Signature Elements**: Ruled line dividers (like notebook paper). Handwritten-style annotation marks. Stamped labels for section identifiers.
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Hover states reveal underlining, like a pen marking important text. Clicks feel like turning a page. Nothing bounces or floats.
- **Animation**: Minimal. Text fades in line by line on scroll, like reading. No parallax. No spinning.
- **Typography System**: Display — Playfair Display Bold for headings (authoritative, earned). Body — IBM Plex Mono for body text (field report, technical, honest). Labels — IBM Plex Sans Condensed for navigation and metadata.

**Idea 2: The Watcher's Interface**

- **Design Movement**: Dark observatory meets governance dashboard — Bauhaus meets surveillance aesthetic
- **Core Principles**: Observation is the primary act. Everything is being watched and watching back. Data and humanity coexist without apology.
- **Color Philosophy**: Deep charcoal (#1A1A2E) base. Electric teal (#00D4AA) as the signal color — the color of a monitor in a dark room. Warm white (#F0EDE8) for text. Red (#E63946) only for warnings and THE CUT.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Grid-based but broken intentionally. Like a monitoring dashboard where one panel is always slightly out of alignment. The asymmetry is the point.
- **Signature Elements**: Thin scan-line texture overlaid on hero sections. Blinking cursor on key phrases. Terminal-style text reveal animations.
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Everything feels like it is being logged. Hover states show a subtle "recording" indicator. The interface watches you interact with it.
- **Animation**: Scan lines. Text types in on load. Subtle pulse on the mascot. The watcher blinks.
- **Typography System**: Display — Space Grotesk Bold (technical, modern, not corporate). Body — Space Mono (terminal, honest, precise). Accent labels — Space Grotesk Medium.

**Idea 3: The Living Document**

- **Design Movement**: Editorial modernism meets children's book warmth — two registers, one system
- **Core Principles**: The site has two modes that coexist: the adult research layer (precise, dark, earned) and the children's layer (warm, illustrated, playful). The transition between them is the design. The sloth lives in both.
- **Color Philosophy**: Primary — Deep slate (#1C2B3A). Secondary — Warm cream (#FAF6EF). Children's accent — Sky blue (#5BB8F5), orange (#F4813F), soft green (#6DBF8A). Adult accent — Amber (#C9882A). The palette shifts when you enter a different audience lens.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Document-style vertical scroll for adult content. Card-grid with illustrated borders for children's content. The navigation is a horizontal ribbon that changes color based on which lens you are in.
- **Signature Elements**: The sloth appears as a watermark/guide throughout. Section dividers use illustrated brush strokes for children's sections, ruled lines for adult sections. Each audience lens has its own color temperature.
- **Interaction Philosophy**: The site responds to who you say you are. Choosing your lens changes the visual register. The watcher watches which door you choose.
- **Animation**: Sloth animates on page load — slow, deliberate, reading. Adult sections: fade and slide. Children's sections: bounce and grow. The transition between registers is a gentle dissolve.
- **Typography System**: Adult — Lora Serif for headings (earned, literary), Inter for body (readable, honest). Children — Nunito Bold for headings (rounded, warm), Nunito Regular for body. The font changes with the lens.

## CHOSEN DESIGN: Idea 3 — The Living Document

**Rationale**: This is the only approach that honors both audiences simultaneously without compromise. The adult layer is dark, precise, and earned. The children's layer is warm, illustrated, and playful. The sloth lives in both. The transition between registers is itself a demonstration of the framework — the site adapts to who is watching, just as the methodology says it should.

**The watcher watches which door you choose. The site responds accordingly.**
